## A simple loop, with better answers

**01 · Log what you eat.** Search local food data, scan a barcode, save a recipe, or use Phase to describe a meal in plain language.

**02 · Log your weight.** MacroPhase smooths daily scale noise into a trend so one unusual morning does not tell the whole story.

**03 · Review and decide.** Your logged intake and weight trend inform an expenditure estimate. Weekly check-ins can suggest a new target; you choose whether to accept it.

## Made for daily use

| Log with less friction | Understand the trend | Keep control |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Barcode, camera, search, recipes, custom foods, favorites, and time-based picks. | Calories and macros, recent micronutrient averages, trend weight, and a rolling expenditure estimate. | Local food and weight records, optional Health Connect data, and target changes you approve. |

Food search starts with data on your device. Open Food Facts can help with packaged foods when you need an online result. Micronutrient coverage depends on the information available for each food.

## Phase, when you want a hand

Phase can work with your log: search foods, help log a meal, review your intake, and discuss trends. **The tracker works without AI.** To use Phase, you configure a supported provider key; conversation context is sent to that provider. [How data and API access work →](https://macrophase.github.io/Docs/#/resources/privacy_api)

## Get the beta

1. Download the Android APK from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/MacroPhase/App/releases/latest).
2. Install it on your phone and complete onboarding.
3. Start logging. An API key is only needed if you want to use Phase.

MacroPhase is currently distributed as an Android beta APK. It is not on the Play Store or iOS, and it does not sync your data between devices.
